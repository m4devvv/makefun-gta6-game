PALMORA BAY

Open-World Crime • Top-Down

Move: WASD / Arrow keys  •  Enter / exit car: E
Drive: W = gas, S = brake/reverse, A/D = steer  •  Pause: P
Missions: 1) Steal a car & deliver it   2) Collect 5 packages   3) Escape a 3-star wanted level
Stealing cars, hitting pedestrians or ramming police raises your wanted level. Hide to cool down.
Progress (cash + mission) auto-saves. Sound: simple WebAudio beeps — click Start to enable.
